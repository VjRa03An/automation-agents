How this projection works

  • Federal tax uses 2026 IRS brackets, standard deduction, the 65+ additions, the 2025–2028 senior deduction, 0/15/20% capital-gains rates, 3.8% NIIT and Social Security taxation. Brackets rise with your inflation rate. State tax is a flat rate on income above the standard deduction.
  • Medicare surcharges (IRMAA) use 2026 CMS tiers and your income from two years earlier, per person on Medicare. Only the surcharge above the standard premium is added; put base premiums in spending.
  • RMDs start at 73 (born 1951–59) or 75 (born 1960+) using the IRS Uniform Lifetime Table. Social Security adjusts for claiming age and includes the 50% spousal top-up.
  • Withdrawals happen at the start of each year; growth applies to what remains. Every account follows the same stock/bond mix.
  • This is a planning tool, not tax, legal or investment advice. Tax rules and thresholds change every year; check current figures and review large decisions with a licensed professional.
